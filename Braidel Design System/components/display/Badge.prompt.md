Small status / metadata pill.

```jsx
<Badge variant="success" dot>Matched</Badge>
<Badge variant="warning">Pending review</Badge>
<Badge variant="brand">Verified braider</Badge>
<Badge variant="gold">Top rated</Badge>
```

Variants: `neutral · brand · gold · success · warning · danger · info · solid · outline`. Add `dot` for a leading status dot. Keep to one or two words.
